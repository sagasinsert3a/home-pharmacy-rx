Patient Medication Access

Learn how Home Pharmacy helps uninsured and underinsured individuals receive vital prescription medications at no or low cost.

Who Is Eligible?

Our mission is to ensure no one goes without life-sustaining medication due to financial hardship. You may qualify for our services if you meet any of the following criteria:

  • You do not have active prescription health insurance coverage (uninsured).
  • You have insurance, but copays, deductibles, or coverage gaps prevent you from affording medications (underinsured).
  • You reside in Greater Orlando or the surrounding Central Florida communities.
  • You have an active, valid prescription written by a licensed healthcare provider.

4 Simple Steps to Get Your Medication

1

Obtain a Prescription From Your Doctor

Ask your doctor, nurse practitioner, or community health clinic to send your prescription to Home Pharmacy in Orlando, FL. Prescriptions can be submitted via e-prescribe, phone at (407) 636-0929, or direct fax.

2

Contact or Visit Home Pharmacy

Call our team at (407) 636-0929 or stop by 717 W Lancaster Rd, Orlando, FL 32809. Our bilingual and compassionate team will guide you through our quick intake registration.

3

Pharmacist Consultation

Our licensed pharmacists conduct a personalized, confidential consultation to ensure you understand your dosages, medication schedules, potential side effects, and chronic disease management.

4

Receive Your Medication & Ongoing Refills

Pick up your medications at no or low cost, and enjoy reliable refill scheduling so your treatment regimen remains continuous and uninterrupted.

Have questions about your prescription?

Call our friendly pharmacy team right now or visit us in person. We are here to support your health journey with dignity.
