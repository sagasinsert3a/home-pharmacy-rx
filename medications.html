Medications & Formulary

We stock essential maintenance medications for chronic conditions to support health equity and long-term wellness.

Commonly Supported Therapeutic Categories

Medication availability fluctuates based on community assistance inventory and donations. Contact us directly at (407) 636-0929 to confirm specific drug strengths and forms.

🫀 Blood Pressure & Heart

Lisinopril, Amlodipine, Losartan, Metoprolol, Hydrochlorothiazide, Carvedilol, and other cardiovascular therapies.

🩸 Diabetes & Endocrine

Metformin, Glipizide, select insulin supplies, testing meters, lancets, and diabetes monitoring essentials.

🫁 Asthma & COPD

Albuterol inhalers, steroid maintenance inhalers, Montelukast, and respiratory nebulizer solutions.

🧬 Cholesterol & Lipids

Atorvastatin, Simvastatin, Rosuvastatin, Pravastatin, and associated lipid-lowering agents.

🦠 Antibiotics & Infections

Amoxicillin, Azithromycin, Cephalexin, Doxycycline, Sulfamethoxazole/Trimethoprim, and antifungal treatments.

🧠 Mental Health & Wellness

Sertraline, Fluoxetine, Escitalopram, Citalopram, Trazodone, and non-controlled emotional well-being medications.

🌾 Allergies & Antihistamines

Cetirizine, Loratadine, Fluticasone nasal sprays, and allergy management solutions.

⚡ Seizures & Neurological

Levetiracetam, Gabapentin (where compliant), Topiramate, and maintenance neurological care.

🍽️ Gastrointestinal / Stomach

Omeprazole, Pantoprazole, Famotidine, Ondansetron, and digestive support medicines.

🦋 Thyroid Regulation

Levothyroxine at various microgram strengths for hypothyroid maintenance.

👩 Women’s Health

Prenatal vitamins, hormone support, and general wellness therapeutics.

👁️ Eye & Ear Care

Glaucoma pressure drops (Latanoprost, Timolol) and antimicrobial ophthalmic drops.

⚠️ Controlled Substances Statement

Home Pharmacy strictly complies with safety regulations and does not stock, carry, or dispense DEA Schedule II, III, IV, or V controlled substances (such as opioids, narcotics, benzodiazepines, or stimulant ADHD medications). Please consult your primary care doctor for non-controlled therapeutic alternatives.
