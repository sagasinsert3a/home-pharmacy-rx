About Home Pharmacy

Dedicated to eliminating financial barriers and ensuring equitable medication access across Central Florida.

Our Mission & Purpose

At Home Pharmacy, we believe that health is a fundamental right, not a luxury. Across our community, thousands of working individuals, elderly residents, and families are forced into the heartbreaking choice between paying rent, buying food, or filling critical prescriptions.

Our charitable medication assistance model bridges this divide by providing essential prescriptions and compassionate, one-on-one clinical counseling to those who are uninsured, underinsured, or experiencing temporary financial crises.

"We do not merely dispense medication — we restore peace of mind, prevent chronic illness complications, and treat every neighbor with the dignity they deserve."

The Principles That Guide Us

🌟 Dignity & Respect

Every patient is greeted without judgment or stigma, in an environment that honors privacy and individual humanity.

🛡️ Clinical Excellence

Safety, dosage accuracy, and interaction screenings are maintained to the highest state board and professional standards.

🤝 Community Partnership

We collaborate closely with free clinics, religious centers, civic charities, and regional health systems to maximize impact.
